# Business Recommendations — Global Superstore

## 1. Pricing Optimization
- Reduce blanket discounts
- Introduce targeted pricing strategies

## 2. Product Strategy
- Scale profitable products
- Review loss-making items
- Discontinue non-performing products

## 3. Returns Reduction
- Improve product quality
- Refine packaging
- Improve logistics

## 4. Regional Strategy
- Invest more in high-performing regions
- Redesign strategy for weak markets

## 5. Profit-Driven Management
- Shift focus from revenue tracking to profit optimization.
